Dating

Why hello there, Dewi here. I’m 29, living in San Francisco, and I’m looking for the person who I’ll spend the rest of my life with.

I want to become a loving husband, a silly father, and a grateful member of a joyful, mischievous and happy family.

I need your help. If you know of any single women who’d be excited to go on a date, and who seems like the kind of person I’m looking for, please introduce us!

The type of person I’m looking for

  • Is excited to run for a train if we’re running late.
  • 23-32yo female.
  • Social: Likes hosting events, bringing people together, and making things happen with friends.
  • Creative: Plays a musical instrument, or sings, or writes, or dances, or has some other creative outlet.
  • Smart: Can solve problems fast, knows a lot of stuff, can challenge and inspire me.
  • Morally serious: Cares about doing good and helping other people.
  • Athletic: Is pretty intense about at least one sport, or has been in the past.
  • Family: Is excited about having 3+ kids, and is open to living in a community with other friends.
  • Not (super) religious.

Some details about me that may be of interest to a potential partner

  • Work: I lead a philanthropic startup called BlueDot.
    • I used to work all the time, but I’m now working more reasonable hours and I’d like to spend more of my time with my future partner.
    • I’d like to spend an entire day together every week, where we hang out, go on adventures, spend time with friends, do fun projects together, etc.
    • This blog post describes what I think a great social life looks like (creating together, instead of consuming alone!).
  • Location: I expect to live in the Bay Area for the foreseeable future, with regular trips to Washington DC.
    • If all goes well for the world, I’d love to live in an Earthship at the foot of a mountain next to a fresh water lake, with many friends living nearby.
  • Family: I’d be excited to get married in 2-5 years, and I’d like to become a father in 3-6 years.
    • I will most likely try to raise my kids speaking Welsh (plus any other languages that my partner speaks), even though I know it’s a pretty cursed endeavour to raise kids speaking a minority language.
    • I want to spend a lot of time with my kids. However, to reduce the amount of pressure on me and my partner, I will pay for a full-time au pair to support with childcare. And I plan for BlueDot’s office to have a crèche.
  • My default preference is to have a monogamous relationship, though in the past I’ve dated people who are polyamorous and I’m open to that as well.
