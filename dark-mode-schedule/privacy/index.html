Dark Mode Schedule privacy policy

Last updated 22 Nov 2026

Dark Mode Schedule is a Chrome extension that turns websites dark at one time of day and light again at another. It collects no data about you. Nothing you browse is sent to me or to anyone else.

What it stores

The extension saves your settings in Chrome’s own storage:

  • the two times you set
  • whether you turned dark mode on or off by hand, until the next switch
  • the names of the sites you told it to leave alone

If you use Chrome sync, Google copies these settings between your own computers, as it does your other Chrome settings. I have no access to them.

What it does on the pages you visit

To make a page dark, the extension reads the page’s colors and styles and changes them. This happens inside your browser. It does not record, keep or send what is on the page.

Some pages load their stylesheets and images from another site. To recolor those, the extension fetches the same files itself, without your cookies. The sites that serve those files see that request the way they see any page load. No other requests are made.

What it does not do

  • It has no analytics, tracking or ads.
  • It has no server and no account.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has none.

Changes and questions
