# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate distinct options for a decision, assess their tradeoffs candidly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before commitment by testing its strongest case, critical assumptions, dissent, failure modes, and falsifiable evidence.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/decide.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, make clear calls, preserve authorized records, and review outcomes without inventing the user’s views.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through recommendation, implementation, verification, and handoff.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape a consequential document through evidence review, answer-dependent question rounds, an alignment ledger, a readiness gate, plain drafting, and a final audit.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search only the sources that matter, at a depth that fits the stakes, and turn the findings into one concise context brief where every claim links to its evidence.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a rigorous Socratic dialogue built on retrieval, explanation, and practical application rather than passive summary.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user’s established voice, verify facts and commitments, and optionally create one reviewable draft in the user’s chosen email system.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-posts.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts that lead with concrete value, fit a defined audience, and remain accurate, defensible, and easy to scan.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based case study post with strong hooks, a review-ready draft, quote-card options, approvals, and a publishing checklist.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/cover-images.md): Create and review eight article-specific cover-image options through five initial concepts and three evidence-based improvements.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the past week and shape the next one: commit to one to three checkable goals, protect capacity, triage tasks, trim meetings, and record a short plan.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-month.md): Close one month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked, explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, and concrete commitments.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): A 10-15 minute rescue for when you are tired, dreading a task, unclear on the next step, or distracted: diagnose the state, run one matched intervention, and start one work block.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, repeatable bedtime workflow that lowers stimulation, puts distractions out of reach, prepares the body, and gives a short reply when sleep does not come.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan a trip around its purpose, compare complete journeys and current fares against the traveler's preferences, and prepare or complete a booking within clear authorization.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/meeting-prep.md): Research the right context, clarify the user’s intended outcome, and produce a focused, access-appropriate meeting brief and agenda.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/meeting-actions.md): Review authorized meeting records, capture genuine unfinished commitments as clear deduplicated tasks, and batch only the judgment calls that need clarification.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/message-to-task.md): Turn a message thread into researched, pre-completed work, and create a clear task record only when tracking will help.
- [Process an inbox to zero](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/inbox-zero.md): Turn authorized email, chat, comment, and messaging inboxes into clear decisions and trusted tasks, one bucket at a time, without confusing triage with doing the work.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that yields job-relevant evidence, scores quickly, and is validated with simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare a reference call for a job candidate by gathering minimal, authorized context and building a briefing page with a tailored opening, briefing notes, and questions.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser tasks safely: pick the least invasive route, verify the account and every edit, and gate irreversible actions behind a readiness check.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-a-skill.md): Create, test, and improve reusable AI skills through realistic tests, human review, objective checks, and activation tuning, then package them for handoff.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify every UI and CSS change across representative screen sizes, realistic content, screenshots, and layout checks before completion.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/recurring-events.md): Create, publish, verify, and invite the approved audience to authorized recurring community events using adaptable tools, privacy-aware controls, and readiness checks.
