# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate distinct options for a decision, assess their tradeoffs honestly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before commitment through a steelman, sequential challenge, pre-mortem, verdict, and concrete next action.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, make authorized records without inventing the user’s views, and review outcomes to improve future choices.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through options, implementation, verification, and handoff, with an analysis-only mode.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents by reviewing evidence, resolving material choices, and checking readiness. Then draft and audit the smallest document that can achieve the goal.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search only the sources that matter, then produce a proportionate, evidence-linked context brief for a person, organization, project, topic, or decision.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a Socratic dialogue that builds recall, reasoning, and practical application instead of passive review.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user’s authentic voice using approved style evidence, verified facts, and a concise final audit.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts with strong hooks, defensible claims, useful substance, and privacy-aware publishing controls.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based case study post about a person’s career, learning, or professional change. The workflow produces a review-ready draft, alternate hooks, quote-card options, and an approval log.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-editorial-cover-images.md): Create eight article-grounded cover-image options through five concepts, visual review, and three evidence-based improvements in any chosen image generator.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Close one month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked and explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, and concrete commitments.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Diagnose why someone is stuck, use one brief matched intervention, start a bounded work block, and learn from the result without making coaching another avoidance ritual.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, interactive evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares essential morning needs offline, and provides a brief stimulus-control response when sleep does not come.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan travel around its purpose, compare complete current journeys, and prepare or complete authorized bookings with clear decisions and privacy-aware research.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Gather the relevant history, clarify the desired outcome, and produce a focused agenda and preparation brief.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review authorized meeting records, identify genuine unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated tasks for confident follow-ups, and batch only questions that require judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read a complete conversation, research and pre-complete safe work, then create a useful task only when tracking the remaining action will help.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and can be scored consistently. Validate that it distinguishes role-relevant performance through simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare and run a concise reference conversation that gathers specific evidence instead of vague praise.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser tasks safely by choosing the least invasive method, protecting authenticated context, verifying page state, and separating preparation from consequential action.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): Design, test, improve, evaluate, and package reusable AI skills through a practical, user-centered iteration workflow.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify UI and CSS changes across representative widths, heights, content states, and target devices using screenshots plus programmatic layout checks before release.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
