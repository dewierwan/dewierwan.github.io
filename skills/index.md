# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate genuinely distinct options for a decision, weigh their tradeoffs honestly, and recommend a short ranked set. Use when someone asks for options, alternatives, or ways they could approach something.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find the weak points in a strategic idea before committing to it, through a steelman, assumption tests, a pre-mortem, and a verdict. Use when someone asks to stress-test an idea or wonders if they are wrong.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/decide.md): Match decision rigor to stakes and reversibility, make a clear call, record it with a prediction, and review it later. Use when someone asks for help deciding, weighing X against Y, or reviewing a past decision.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial build or process problem from diagnosis through options, recommendation, implementation, and verification. Use when someone wants a problem solved end to end, or analysis only before building.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape a high-stakes document through evidence review and rounds of questions until the thinking is settled, then draft and audit it. Use when writing a strategy doc, proposal, or decision memo that must land.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources that matter, at a depth that fits the stakes, and turn the findings into one brief where every claim links to evidence. Use when someone wants to get up to speed on a person, project, or decision.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Teach a paper, article, or topic through Socratic questions and retrieval practice instead of passive summary. Use when someone says teach me this, help me understand it, or quiz me on it.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user's own voice, built from real samples, with facts and commitments checked before anything is sent. Use when someone asks to write, reply to, or polish an email.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-posts.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts that lead with concrete value and stay accurate and easy to scan. Use when someone wants to write a post, promote their work, or improve a draft.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Turn a real person's career or learning story into an evidence-based case study post, with hooks, quote cards, and an approval checklist. Use when someone wants to feature a person's journey publicly.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/cover-images.md): Create cover images for articles and newsletters through five concepts, a close look at the results, and three improved versions. Use when someone needs a header image or an image-generator prompt for a post.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Chooses one most important outcome for the day, protects time for it, and plans around real commitments. Use when someone says 'plan my day', 'plan tomorrow', or asks what to focus on.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what got done against the plan, what mattered, and what to carry forward. Use when someone wants to review their day, rate it, or offload worries before bed.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review last week, commit to a few outcomes with a clear definition of done, cut low-value work, and protect time. Use when someone wants to plan the coming week or reset after a messy one.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-month.md): Close one month honestly, then plan the next with a capacity check, a NOT list, and concrete commitments. Use when a month is ending, or when someone wants a monthly review or plan.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Diagnose why someone is stuck, then run one short, matched intervention to get the first work block started. Use when someone feels tired, dreads a task, cannot get started, or keeps getting distracted.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): Guide a short evening routine that closes open loops, lowers stimulation, and prepares the morning, with a fallback for sleepless nights. Use when someone says they want to wind down or cannot sleep.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan a trip around its purpose, compare complete journeys against the traveler's preferences, and book within clear limits. Use when someone needs flights, trains, or transfers found or booked.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/meeting-prep.md): Research the people and history behind a meeting, clarify the outcome wanted, and produce a focused brief and agenda. Use when someone says prep my meeting with a person, or before an important call.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/meeting-actions.md): Review meeting notes or transcripts, capture real unfinished commitments as clear tasks, and batch only the unclear ones as questions. Use when someone asks what they committed to or wants meeting follow-ups.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/message-to-task.md): Turn a message thread into researched, partly completed work, and create a task only when tracking will help. Use when someone shares a message and says make this a task, track this, or deal with it.
- [Process an inbox to zero](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/inbox-zero.md): Work through an email or message inbox to zero, turning each item into a clear next action without drifting into deep work. Use when someone wants to triage, clear, or prioritize an inbox.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Design a short, paid, take-home work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and is tested with simulated submissions. Use when someone is hiring and needs a work test or wants to improve one.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare a focused reference call for a job candidate, with a briefing page, an opening, and questions that draw out specific evidence. Use when someone has a reference call coming up.
- [Check before granting access](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/access-check.md): Before someone joins a shared chat workspace or knowledge base, find content about them they could read and recommend what to restrict. Use when adding a new hire, advisor, or collaborator.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser tasks safely: pick the least invasive route, protect logged-in accounts, check what the page actually shows, and confirm before acting. Use when filling forms or fixing dashboards in a browser.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-a-skill.md): Design, test, and improve a reusable AI skill, from a rough idea or a repeated workflow to a tested, well-triggered skill. Use when someone wants to turn a workflow into a skill or improve one.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Check every UI or CSS change across narrow, wide, short, and tall screens with screenshots and layout checks before calling it done. Use before shipping any front-end change.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/recurring-events.md): Publish the next date of a recurring community event, refresh its image and copy, and invite people who opted in. Use when someone says set up this week's event or post the next one.
