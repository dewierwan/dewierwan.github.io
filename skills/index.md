# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate distinct options for a decision or problem, assess tradeoffs candidly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before commitment through steelmanning, sequential challenge, a pre-mortem, and a clear verdict with next action.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision rigor to stakes and reversibility, make and record meaningful choices with permission, and review predictions without inventing the user’s views.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through options, implementation, verification, and handoff, with an analysis-only mode.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents through evidence review, answer-dependent alignment rounds, readiness checks, concise drafting, and a final audit.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources that are likely to matter and turn the findings into one concise, well-sourced brief.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a short Socratic dialogue using retrieval, explanation, challenge, and application rather than passive summary.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user’s authentic voice using authorized style evidence, accurate facts, and a concise final audit.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts from source material using strong hooks, supported claims, useful substance, and targeted editing.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based case study post with verified story beats, alternate hooks, approved quote-card options, and a clear reader action.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-editorial-cover-images.md): Create cover images from an article through five distinct concepts, visual review, and three informed improvements using the chosen image generator.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Review a month with evidence, identify structural lessons, and create a small, capacity-checked next-month plan that the user explicitly approves.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, interactive evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares essential morning needs offline, and provides a brief stimulus-control response when sleep does not come.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan travel around the trip’s purpose, compare complete current journeys, and prepare or make authorized bookings with clear checks, privacy limits, and practical trade-offs.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Gather the relevant history, clarify the desired outcome, and produce a focused agenda and preparation brief.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review authorized meeting records, identify genuine unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated follow-up tasks, and batch only questions requiring judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read a message thread, prepare the real work, and create a trackable task only when a record will help complete it safely.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces role-relevant evidence, is practical to score, and is validated with simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare, conduct, and document a fair, privacy-respecting hiring reference call that gathers concrete, role-relevant evidence for a hiring decision.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser tasks safely by using the least invasive method, protecting account context, verifying rendered state, and separating preparation from commitment.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): Design, test, improve, evaluate, and package reusable AI skills from a new idea, an existing draft, or a demonstrated workflow.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify every UI or CSS change across representative widths, heights, content states, screenshots, and layout checks before reporting it complete.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
