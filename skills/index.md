# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate genuinely distinct options for a decision or problem, assess their tradeoffs candidly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before committing: strengthen the case, test its load-bearing assumptions through sequential challenge, and finish with a clear verdict, handoff, and concrete next action.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, then make, record, and review meaningful choices without inventing the user’s views or exposing sensitive information.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through options, recommendation, implementation, verification, and handoff. The workflow supports analysis-only work when requested and requires explicit,.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents by reviewing authorized evidence, resolving material choices through answer-dependent interviews, checking readiness, and then drafting and auditing the smallest document that can achieve the goal.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources most likely to matter and turn the findings into a concise, evidence-linked context brief for a decision, task, or conversation, while scaling research to the stakes and respecting authorization and privacy limits.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, post, or topic through a short Socratic dialogue that emphasizes retrieval, explanation, challenge, and application rather than passive summary.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user's established voice by applying an approved writing profile, verified facts, and a concise final audit. Keep the result copy-ready, accurate, appropriately bounded, and suited to the recipient and purpose.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts from notes, drafts, articles, transcripts, research, or a topic, emphasizing evidence-backed specificity, strong hooks, useful substance, respectful audience targeting, and targeted edits.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based case study post about a person’s career, learning, or professional change. The workflow produces a review-ready draft, alternate hooks, quote-card options, and an approval log.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Close a month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked, explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, concrete commitments, and a reusable review record.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, interactive evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares essential morning needs offline, and provides a brief stimulus-control response when sleep does not come.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Gather authorized meeting context, confirm the meeting owner’s intended outcome before assuming an agenda, and create a practical preparation record with an in-call cheat sheet and appropriate follow-up.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review authorized meeting records, identify genuine unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated tasks for confident follow-ups, and batch only the questions that require user judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read a message or conversation, identify the real remaining action, research only the context needed, and pre-complete as much work as is safe before deciding whether a task record is useful.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and can be scored consistently. Validate that it distinguishes role-relevant performance through simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare and run a concise reference conversation that gathers specific evidence instead of vague praise.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser-based tasks safely by choosing the least invasive authorized method, protecting account context and privacy, verifying rendered page state, and separating preparation from consequential final actions.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): A complete, tool-independent workflow for designing, testing, improving, evaluating, and packaging reusable AI skills from new ideas, existing instructions, or demonstrated workflows. It supports lightweight collaboration as well as rigor.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify every UI and CSS change across representative narrow, wide, short, and tall viewports using real screenshots and programmatic layout checks. Treat a change as complete only after failures are fixed and the relevant sweep is rerun.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
