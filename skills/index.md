# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate distinct options for a decision or problem, assess their tradeoffs honestly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find the weak points in a leading strategic idea before committing to it. Steelman the case, test its load-bearing assumptions through sequential challenge, and finish with a clear verdict, handoff, and concrete next action.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, then make, record, and review meaningful choices without inventing the user’s views or exposing sensitive information.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through options, implementation, verification, and handoff. The workflow supports analysis-only work when requested.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents by reviewing evidence, resolving material choices, and checking readiness. Then draft and audit the smallest document that can achieve the goal.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources most likely to matter, then turn the findings into a concise, evidence-linked context brief proportionate to the user’s purpose, authorization, and stakes.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a short Socratic dialogue that uses retrieval, explanation, and application instead of passive summary.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft email in the user's real voice while keeping facts, commitments, and tone appropriate. Use a style guide or recent sent messages as evidence, then audit voice and accuracy.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts from notes, drafts, articles, transcripts, or a topic. The workflow emphasizes concrete claims, strong hooks, useful substance, and targeted revision.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based case study post about a person’s career, learning, or professional change. The workflow produces a review-ready draft, alternate hooks, quote-card options, and an approval log.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Close one month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked and explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, and concrete commitments.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, interactive evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares essential morning needs offline, and provides a brief stimulus-control response when sleep does not come.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Gather the relevant history, clarify the desired outcome, and produce a focused agenda and preparation brief.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review meeting records, identify genuine unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated tasks for confident follow-ups, and batch only questions that require judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read a message or conversation, identify the real outcome, prepare as much work as safely possible, and create a task only when durable tracking will help. Keep research, drafts, records, and follow-up within legitimate access boundaries.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and can be scored consistently. Validate that it distinguishes role-relevant performance through simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare and run a concise reference conversation that gathers specific evidence instead of vague praise.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser-based tasks safely by choosing the least invasive authorized method, protecting account context, verifying rendered page state, and separating preparation from consequential commitment.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): A complete workflow for designing, testing, improving, and packaging a reusable AI skill from a new idea, an existing draft, or a repeated workflow captured from a conversation.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify UI and CSS changes across representative narrow, wide, short, and tall viewports. Combine screenshots with programmatic layout checks, then fix and retest every failure.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
