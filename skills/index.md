# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate genuinely distinct paths for a decision, assess tradeoffs candidly, and present a small ranked set without forcing a choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Test a leading strategic idea before committing by examining its assumptions, evidence, dissent, failure modes, and conditions for changing course.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, make clear commitments, preserve authorized records, and learn from outcomes without inventing the user’s views.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through options, implementation, verification, and handoff, with an analysis-only option.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents through evidence review, answer-dependent alignment, readiness checks, drafting, and delivery audit.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources that are likely to matter and turn the findings into one concise, well-sourced brief.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a short Socratic dialogue that builds recall, explanation, and application instead of passive review.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft concise, copy-ready emails in the user’s authentic voice using authorized style evidence, verified facts, and a final tone and accuracy check.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts that lead with useful evidence, clear judgment, and a strong reader-focused hook.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create an evidence-based, approval-ready case study post with alternate hooks, quote-card options, a clear CTA, and a privacy-aware verification process.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-editorial-cover-images.md): Create, inspect, and refine eight article-specific editorial cover images using a chosen image generator and an evidence-based two-round workflow.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Close one month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked and explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, and concrete commitments.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, interactive evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares essential morning needs offline, and provides a brief stimulus-control response when sleep does not come.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan travel around its purpose, compare complete current journeys, and prepare or make authorized bookings while protecting privacy and handling trade-offs clearly.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Research authorized context, clarify the meeting owner’s intent, and create a focused, time-bound meeting brief, agenda, and follow-up plan.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review authorized meeting records, identify genuine unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated tasks, and batch only the questions that require judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read the full conversation, identify the real outcome, prepare safe drafts and research, and create a trackable task only when tracking adds value.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and can be scored consistently. Validate that it distinguishes role-relevant performance through simulated submissions.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare, conduct, and document an authorized hiring reference call using role-relevant evidence, tailored questions, and clear decision boundaries.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser-based tasks safely by choosing the least invasive method, protecting account context, verifying page state, and separating preparation from commitment.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): Design, test, improve, evaluate, and package a reusable AI skill from a new idea, an existing draft, or a demonstrated workflow.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify UI and CSS changes across representative widths, heights, realistic content states, screenshots, and layout checks before release.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
