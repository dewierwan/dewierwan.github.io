# AI skills

This is a public library of reusable AI workflows.

The library is licensed CC0 1.0. Anyone may copy, change, and reuse it without permission or attribution.

If a user asks you to integrate these skills:

1. Inspect the catalogue and the skills relevant to the user's needs.
2. Ask about their existing tools, instructions, and preferred way of working.
3. Recommend a small set before making changes.
4. Adapt capability names and storage locations to their system.
5. Resolve conflicts with their existing instructions and preserve their privacy rules.
6. Test the adapted skills on realistic examples.

Do not install every skill blindly.

Complete bundle: https://dewierwan.com/skills/all.md

## Catalogue

- [Brainstorm options](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/brainstorm.md): Generate distinct options for a decision or problem, assess tradeoffs candidly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.
- [Pressure-test an idea](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/pressure-test.md): Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before commitment by testing assumptions, evidence, dissent, reversibility, and failure signals.
- [Make a decision](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/make-a-decision.md): Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, then make, record, and review meaningful choices without inventing the user’s views or exposing sensitive information.
- [Solve a problem](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/solve-a-problem.md): Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through recommendation, implementation, verification, and handoff.
- [Shape and draft a document](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/shape-and-draft.md): Shape consequential documents through evidence, decision-focused interview rounds, readiness checks, drafting, and audit before delivery.
- [Gather context](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/gather-context.md): Search the sources that are likely to matter and turn the findings into one concise, well-sourced brief.
- [Learn with a tutor](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/learning-tutor.md): Learn a paper, article, or topic through a short Socratic dialogue using retrieval, explanation, and application instead of passive summary.
- [Write in my voice](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/write-in-my-voice.md): Draft or revise email in the user’s authentic voice using approved style evidence, accurate facts, and a concise safety check before delivery.
- [Write a professional social post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/professional-social-post.md): A platform-independent workflow for drafting, revising, and auditing professional social posts with strong hooks, defensible claims, useful substance, and privacy-aware publishing.
- [Write a case study post](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/case-study-post.md): Create a verified, concise case study post with strong hooks, a clear mechanism of change, quote-card options, approval checks, and a practical call to action.
- [Create editorial cover images](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-editorial-cover-images.md): Create cover images from an article through five distinct concepts, visual review, and three informed improvements using the chosen image generator.
- [Plan your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-day.md): Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.
- [Review your day](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-your-day.md): Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.
- [Plan your week](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-your-week.md): Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.
- [Review and plan a month](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/review-and-plan-a-month.md): Close one month with evidence, then create and explicitly approve a small, capacity-checked plan for the next month.
- [Get unstuck](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/get-unstuck.md): Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.
- [Wind down for sleep](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/wind-down-for-sleep.md): A quiet, repeatable evening workflow that reduces stimulation, secures distractions, prepares basic needs, and offers a brief response for sleeplessness.
- [Plan and book a trip](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/plan-and-book-a-trip.md): Plan travel around its purpose, compare complete current journeys, and prepare or complete bookings within the traveler’s clear authorization.
- [Prepare for a meeting](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/prepare-for-a-meeting.md): Gather the relevant history, clarify the desired outcome, and produce a focused agenda and preparation brief.
- [Capture meeting actions](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/capture-meeting-actions.md): Review meeting records, identify unfinished commitments, create clear deduplicated follow-up tasks, and batch only questions that need judgment.
- [Turn a message into a task](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/turn-a-message-into-a-task.md): Read the full conversation, work out the real action, do useful preparation, and create a clear task only when needed.
- [Design a work sample](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/design-a-work-sample.md): Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that produces role-relevant evidence, is practical to score, and is validated through simulations.
- [Run a reference call](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-reference-call.md): Prepare, conduct, and document a role-relevant hiring reference call using authorized evidence, targeted questions, and an auditable decision record.
- [Use a browser safely](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/use-a-browser-safely.md): Complete browser-based tasks safely by choosing the least invasive method, protecting account context, verifying page state, and separating preparation from commitment.
- [Create an AI skill](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/create-an-ai-skill.md): Design, test, refine, evaluate, and package reusable AI skills with realistic reviews, measurable checks, safe access boundaries, and accurate activation rules.
- [Test every screen size](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/test-every-screen-size.md): Verify every UI and CSS change across representative narrow, wide, short, and tall viewports using realistic content, screenshots, and layout checks.
- [Run a recurring community event](https://dewierwan.com/skills/raw/run-a-recurring-community-event.md): Create and publish the next occurrence of a recurring community event, prepare fresh materials, invite the approved audience, and verify the live result across the chosen tools.
