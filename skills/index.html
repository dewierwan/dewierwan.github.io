AI skills

Think and decide

  • Brainstorm options

    Generate genuinely distinct options for a decision, weigh their tradeoffs honestly, and recommend a short ranked set. Use when someone asks for options, alternatives, or ways they could approach something.

  • Pressure-test an idea

    Find the weak points in a strategic idea before committing to it, through a steelman, assumption tests, a pre-mortem, and a verdict. Use when someone asks to stress-test an idea or wonders if they are wrong.

  • Make a decision

    Match decision rigor to stakes and reversibility, make a clear call, record it with a prediction, and review it later. Use when someone asks for help deciding, weighing X against Y, or reviewing a past decision.

  • Solve a problem

    Take a non-trivial build or process problem from diagnosis through options, recommendation, implementation, and verification. Use when someone wants a problem solved end to end, or analysis only before building.

Research and write

  • Shape and draft a document

    Shape a high-stakes document through evidence review and rounds of questions until the thinking is settled, then draft and audit it. Use when writing a strategy doc, proposal, or decision memo that must land.

  • Gather context

    Searches the sources that matter at a depth fitting the stakes and builds one brief where every claim links to evidence. Use when someone says 'gather context on X' or 'get me up to speed.'

  • Learn with a tutor

    Teach a paper, article, or topic through Socratic questions and retrieval practice instead of passive summary. Use when someone says teach me this, help me understand it, or quiz me on it.

  • Write in my voice

    Drafts or revises email in your own voice from real samples, with facts and commitments checked. Use when you say "write this email," "reply to this thread," or "polish my draft."

  • Write a professional social post

    Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts that lead with concrete value and stay accurate and easy to scan. Use when someone wants to write a post, promote their work, or improve a draft.

  • Write a case study post

    Turn a real person's career or learning story into an evidence-based case study post, with hooks, quote cards, and an approval checklist. Use when someone wants to feature a person's journey publicly.

  • Create editorial cover images

    Creates editorial cover images for articles: five distinct concepts, a close review of results, then three improved versions. Use when someone needs a post header image or an image prompt.

Plan and reflect

  • Plan your day

    Turns a crowded or unclear day into one named outcome, protected time, and a NOT today list. Use when someone says 'plan my day', 'plan tomorrow', or asks what to focus on.

  • Review your day

    Runs a short end-of-day reflection on plan vs reality, a win, a drain, and tomorrow's worries. Use when someone says 'review my day', wants to rate their day, or needs to offload before bed.

  • Plan your week

    Reviews last week, commits to one to three goals with checkable outputs, cuts low-value work, and protects time. Use when someone says "plan my week" or wants to reset after a messy week.

  • Review and plan a month

    Closes one month honestly, then plans the next with a capacity check, a NOT list, and concrete commitments. Use when a month is ending or someone asks for a monthly review or month plan.

  • Get unstuck

    Diagnose why someone is stuck, then run one short, matched intervention to get the first work block started. Use when someone feels tired, dreads a task, cannot get started, or keeps getting distracted.

  • Wind down for sleep

    Guides a short evening routine that lowers stimulation, prepares the body and morning, and handles sleepless nights. Use when someone says "wind down", "ready for bed", or "can't sleep".

  • Plan and book a trip

    Plan a trip around its purpose, compare complete journeys against the traveler's preferences, and book within clear limits. Use when someone needs flights, trains, or transfers found or booked.

Meetings and tasks

  • Prepare for a meeting

    Researches a meeting's people and history, asks what outcome you want, then builds a focused brief and agenda. Use when you say prep my meeting with someone or before an important call.

  • Capture meeting actions

    Reviews meeting records, turns real unfinished commitments into clear tasks, and batches unclear ones as questions. Use when asking what you committed to today or for meeting follow-ups.

  • Turn a message into a task

    Turns a message thread into researched, partly completed work and creates a task only when tracking helps. Use when someone says make this a task, track this, or deal with this message.

  • Process an inbox to zero

    Works through email, chat, and notification inboxes to zero, turning each item into one clear next step. Use when someone says triage my inbox, clear my messages, or help me prioritize email.

Hiring and people

  • Design a work sample

    Design a short, paid, take-home work sample that produces job-relevant evidence and is tested with simulated submissions. Use when someone is hiring and needs a work test or wants to improve one.

  • Run a reference call

    Prepares a private briefing page for a candidate reference call, with context, an opening, and targeted questions. Use when checking references or prepping a reference call.

  • Check before granting access

    Finds content about an incoming person in shared chat and knowledge spaces and recommends what to restrict first. Use when adding a new hire or advisor, or checking before you add someone.

Build and automate

  • Use a browser safely

    Completes browser tasks safely: least invasive route, verified account and fields, confirmation before acting. Use when filling forms, fixing dashboards, or working in a signed-in app.

  • Create an AI skill

    Designs, tests, and improves a reusable AI skill from a rough idea or repeated workflow. Use when someone says "turn this into a skill," "improve this skill," or "does my skill help?"

  • Test every screen size

    Check every UI or CSS change across narrow, wide, short, and tall screens with screenshots and layout checks before calling it done. Use before shipping any front-end change.

  • Run a recurring community event

    Sets up the next date of a recurring community event: fresh poster, event page, and invites to opted-in past guests. Use when someone says set up this week's event or post the next one.
