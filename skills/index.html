AI skills

Think and decide

  • Brainstorm options

    Generate genuinely different options, compare them honestly, and choose the strongest ones.

  • Pressure-test an idea

    Find the weak points in an idea before committing to it. Test assumptions, objections, and likely failure modes.

  • Make a decision

    Match the amount of analysis to the stakes, compare the real options, and record what would change the decision.

  • Solve a problem

    Take a messy problem from diagnosis through research, options, recommendation, and implementation.

Research and write

  • Shape and draft a document

    Develop consequential documents by shaping the underlying decisions before drafting. This workflow uses evidence, successive answer-dependent interview rounds, an alignment ledger, a readiness gate, and a final audit.

  • Gather context

    Search the sources that are likely to matter and turn the findings into one concise, well-sourced brief.

  • Learn with a tutor

    Learn a topic through short explanations, active recall, and questions that adjust to your understanding.

  • Write in my voice

    Use a personal style guide to draft messages that sound like the user instead of generic AI writing.

  • Write a professional social post

    Turn notes or source material into a clear social post with a strong opening and a specific point.

  • Write a case study post

    Turn a person's real change into a short, evidence-based case study without exaggerating their story.

Plan and reflect

  • Plan your day

    Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.

  • Review your day

    Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.

  • Plan your week

    Review the previous week, choose a few outcomes, cut lower-value work, and protect time for what matters.

  • Review and plan a month

    Close one month honestly, choose a theme for the next, and turn it into a small set of concrete commitments.

  • Get unstuck

    Work out why you are stuck, then use a short intervention suited to tiredness, dread, confusion, or distraction.

  • Wind down for sleep

    Guide a short evening routine that closes open loops, prepares the environment, and makes sleep easier.

Meetings and tasks

  • Prepare for a meeting

    Gather the relevant history, clarify the desired outcome, and produce a focused agenda and preparation brief.

  • Capture meeting actions

    Review meeting notes, identify genuine commitments, create clear tasks, and ask about uncertain cases.

  • Turn a message into a task

    Read the full conversation, work out the real action, do useful preparation, and create a clear task only when needed.

Hiring and people

  • Design a work sample

    Create a short paid exercise that tests the real work of a role and distinguishes strong from weak performance.

  • Run a reference call

    Prepare and run a concise reference conversation that gathers specific evidence instead of vague praise.

Build and automate

  • Use a browser safely

    Control websites carefully, inspect before acting, verify every change, and separate preparation from final submission.

  • Create an AI skill

    Turn a repeated workflow into a focused skill, test when it triggers, and improve it using real examples.

  • Test every screen size

    Check a user interface across narrow, wide, short, and tall screens using screenshots and numerical checks.

  • Run a recurring community event

    Create a repeatable weekly event, prepare its materials, invite the right people, and verify every public detail.
