AI skills

Think and decide

  • Brainstorm options

    Generate distinct options for a decision, assess their tradeoffs candidly, and recommend a short ranked set without forcing a final choice.

  • Pressure-test an idea

    Find weak points in a leading strategic idea before commitment by testing its strongest case, critical assumptions, dissent, failure modes, and falsifiable evidence.

  • Make a decision

    Match decision-making rigor to stakes and reversibility, make clear calls, preserve authorized records, and review outcomes without inventing the user’s views.

  • Solve a problem

    Take a non-trivial product, technical, process, or automation problem from diagnosis through recommendation, implementation, verification, and handoff.

Research and write

  • Shape and draft a document

    Shape a consequential document through evidence review, answer-dependent question rounds, an alignment ledger, a readiness gate, plain drafting, and a final audit.

  • Gather context

    Search only the sources that matter, at a depth that fits the stakes, and turn the findings into one concise context brief where every claim links to its evidence.

  • Learn with a tutor

    Learn a paper, article, or topic through a rigorous Socratic dialogue built on retrieval, explanation, and practical application rather than passive summary.

  • Write in my voice

    Draft or revise email in the user’s established voice, verify facts and commitments, and optionally create one reviewable draft in the user’s chosen email system.

  • Write a professional social post

    Draft, revise, and audit professional social posts that lead with concrete value, fit a defined audience, and remain accurate, defensible, and easy to scan.

  • Write a case study post

    Create an evidence-based case study post with strong hooks, a review-ready draft, quote-card options, approvals, and a publishing checklist.

  • Create editorial cover images

    Create and review eight article-specific cover-image options through five initial concepts and three evidence-based improvements.

Plan and reflect

  • Plan your day

    Choose one most important outcome, protect time for it, and make a realistic plan around existing commitments.

  • Review your day

    Run a short end-of-day reflection on what happened, what mattered, and what should change tomorrow.

  • Plan your week

    Review the past week and shape the next one: commit to one to three checkable goals, protect capacity, triage tasks, trim meetings, and record a short plan.

  • Review and plan a month

    Close one month honestly, then create a small, capacity-checked, explicitly approved plan for the next month using evidence, trade-offs, and concrete commitments.

  • Get unstuck

    A 10-15 minute rescue for when you are tired, dreading a task, unclear on the next step, or distracted: diagnose the state, run one matched intervention, and start one work block.

  • Wind down for sleep

    A quiet, repeatable bedtime workflow that lowers stimulation, puts distractions out of reach, prepares the body, and gives a short reply when sleep does not come.

  • Plan and book a trip

    Plan a trip around its purpose, compare complete journeys and current fares against the traveler's preferences, and prepare or complete a booking within clear authorization.

Meetings and tasks

  • Prepare for a meeting

    Prepare one meeting: research proportionately, post a situation brief, ask targeted questions, then build an access-appropriate page with a goal, agenda, and close.

  • Capture meeting actions

    Review authorized meeting records, capture genuine unfinished commitments as clear deduplicated tasks, and batch only the judgment calls that need clarification.

  • Turn a message into a task

    Turn a message thread into researched, pre-completed work, and create a clear task record only when tracking will help.

  • Process an inbox to zero

    Triage authorized email, chat, and comment inboxes into five buckets and one trusted task list, one batch at a time, without doing the work the messages describe.

Hiring and people

  • Design a work sample

    Create or improve a short, paid, asynchronous work sample that yields job-relevant evidence, scores quickly, and is validated with simulated submissions.

  • Run a reference call

    Prepare a reference call for a job candidate by gathering minimal, authorized context and building a briefing page with a tailored opening, briefing notes, and questions.

  • Check before granting access

    Before giving someone access to a shared chat workspace or knowledge base, find content about them they could read and recommend what to restrict.

Build and automate

  • Use a browser safely

    Complete browser tasks safely: pick the least invasive route, verify the account and every edit, and gate irreversible actions behind a readiness check.

  • Create an AI skill

    Create, test, and improve reusable AI skills through realistic tests, human review, objective checks, and activation tuning, then package them for handoff.

  • Test every screen size

    Verify every UI and CSS change across representative screen sizes, realistic content, screenshots, and layout checks before completion.

  • Run a recurring community event

    Create, publish, verify, and invite the approved audience to authorized recurring community events using adaptable tools, privacy-aware controls, and readiness checks.
