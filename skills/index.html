AI skills

Think and decide

  • Brainstorm options

    Generate genuinely distinct options for a decision, weigh their tradeoffs honestly, and recommend a short ranked set. Use when someone asks for options, alternatives, or ways they could approach something.

  • Pressure-test an idea

    Finds the weak points in a strategic idea before you commit, through a steelman, assumption tests, a pre-mortem, and a verdict. Use when you say pressure-test this, stress-test this, or am I wrong.

  • Make a decision

    Matches decision rigor to stakes and reversibility, records the call with a testable prediction, and reviews it later. Use when asked to help decide, weigh X against Y, or review a past decision.

  • Solve a problem

    Takes a non-trivial build or process problem from diagnosis to options, recommendation, and implementation. Use when someone says "build X" or "think it through, don't build yet".

Research and write

  • Shape and draft a document

    Shapes a high-stakes document through evidence review and rounds of questions, then drafts and audits it. Use when writing a strategy doc, proposal, or decision memo that must land.

  • Gather context

    Searches the right sources at a depth fitting the stakes and builds one brief where every claim links to evidence. Use when asked to gather context on X or get someone up to speed.

  • Learn with a tutor

    Teaches a paper, article, or topic through Socratic questions and retrieval practice instead of passive summary. Use when someone says teach me this, help me understand it, or quiz me on it.

  • Write in my voice

    Drafts or revises email in your own voice from real samples, with facts and commitments checked. Use when you say "write this email," "reply to this thread," or "polish my draft."

  • Write a professional social post

    Drafts, revises, and audits professional social posts that lead with concrete, accurate value. Use when someone says "make this into a post," wants hook options, or needs to promote an article.

  • Write a case study post

    Turns interview notes about a person's career change into a verified case study post with hooks and quote cards. Use when asked for an alumni story, success post, or participant feature.

  • Create editorial cover images

    Creates editorial cover images for articles: five distinct concepts, a close review, then three improved versions. Use when someone needs a post header, article cover, or image prompt.

Plan and reflect

  • Plan your day

    Turns a crowded or unclear day into one named outcome, protected time, and a NOT today list. Use when someone says 'plan my day', 'plan tomorrow', or asks what to focus on.

  • Review your day

    Runs a short end-of-day reflection on plan vs reality, a win, a drain, and tomorrow's worries. Use when someone says 'review my day', wants to rate their day, or needs to offload before bed.

  • Plan your week

    Reviews last week, then shapes the next one into one to three checkable goals, tasks, time blocks, and a one-screen plan. Use when someone says "plan my week" or wants to reset after a messy week.

  • Review and plan a month

    Reviews the month ending with evidence, then plans the next with a theme, capacity math, cuts, and sign-off. Use when a month is ending or someone says "monthly review" or "plan my month".

  • Get unstuck

    Diagnoses why someone is stuck, then runs one short, matched intervention to start a first work block. Use when someone says "I can't get started", "I'm dreading this", or "I'm exhausted".

  • Wind down for sleep

    Guides a short evening routine that lowers stimulation, prepares the body and morning, and handles sleepless nights. Use when someone says "wind down", "ready for bed", or "can't sleep".

  • Plan and book a trip

    Plans a trip around its purpose, compares complete door-to-door journeys, and books within clear limits. Use when someone says "find me flights", "plan my trip", or "book the train".

Meetings and tasks

  • Prepare for a meeting

    Researches a meeting's context, asks what outcome you want, then builds a focused brief and agenda page. Use when you say prep my meeting with someone or before an important call.

  • Capture meeting actions

    Reviews meeting records, turns real unfinished commitments into clear tasks, and batches unclear ones as questions. Use when asking what you committed to today or for meeting follow-ups.

  • Turn a message into a task

    Turns a message thread into researched, partly completed work and files or updates a task only when tracking helps. Use when someone says make this a task, track this, or deal with this message.

  • Process an inbox to zero

    Works email, chat, and notification inboxes to zero, giving each item one bucket and a next step. Use when someone says triage my inbox, clear my messages, or help me prioritize email.

Hiring and people

  • Design a work sample

    Designs a short, paid take-home work sample and tests it with simulated candidate submissions. Use when someone says "design a work test", "make a take-home", or "tighten our work test".

  • Run a reference call

    Prepares a briefing page for a candidate reference call, with context, a tailored opening, and targeted questions. Use when checking references or asked to prep a reference call.

  • Check before granting access

    Finds content about an incoming person in shared chat and knowledge spaces and recommends what to restrict first. Use when adding a new hire or advisor, or checking before you add someone.

Build and automate

  • Use a browser safely

    Completes browser tasks safely: least invasive route, verified account and fields, confirmation before acting. Use when filling forms, fixing dashboards, or working in a signed-in app.

  • Create an AI skill

    Designs, tests, and improves a reusable AI skill from a rough idea or repeated workflow. Use when someone says "turn this into a skill," "improve this skill," or "does my skill help?"

  • Test every screen size

    Check every UI or CSS change across narrow, wide, short, and tall screens with screenshots and layout checks before calling it done. Use before shipping any front-end change.

  • Run a recurring community event

    Sets up the next date of a recurring community event: fresh poster, event page, and invites to opted-in past guests. Use when someone says set up this week's event or post the next one.
